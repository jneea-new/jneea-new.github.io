    Call for papers

    Abstract.

    We kindly invite colleagues working on subjects related to evolution equations to submit a paper to our Journal of Nonlinear Evolution Equations and Applications (JNEEA). Your paper will contribute to the success of JNEEA.

    If you accept our invitation, please prepare your manuscript according to the instructions for authors.

    Papers submitted to the journal are refereed within 2-3 months. They undergo initial screening by Editors and anonymous refereeing by at least two independent experts. Upon acceptance they are published in less than 3 months.
